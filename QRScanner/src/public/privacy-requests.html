Privacy Requests

When a customer asks what you have about them, or asks to be erased. Erasing (redacting) replaces their name, email and phone everywhere but keeps every order and ticket, so your sales, refunds and box-office reports stay correct. Their tickets keep working. Records of their consent to texts are kept as legal proof, and any texts to them stop.

Answer within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (California). Confirm the request really came from the person before you act, for example by replying to the email address on their order.

Fill in either one, or both if they gave both.
