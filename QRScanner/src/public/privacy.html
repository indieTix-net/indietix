Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20th, 2026

Your Venue Inc. ("Your Venue," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Your Venue ticketing website and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at the address at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you purchase a ticket or sign up for text notifications:

  • Contact information: your name, email address, and (if you opt in to text notifications) your mobile phone number.
  • Purchase information: what you bought, when, and payment details. Payment card details are processed by Square and are not stored on our systems.
  • Consent records: when you opt in to text notifications, we record the date, time, IP address, browser, and exact consent language you agreed to. This is required by wireless carrier regulations.

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit our site, including your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. This helps us operate the site and troubleshoot problems.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver your ticket and send purchase confirmations.
  • Send a text notification you have opted into — ticket delivery for events you have purchased.
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
  • Improve our services and prevent fraud.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging

We do not sell, rent, share, or otherwise disclose your mobile phone number, or the fact that you have opted in to receive text messages, to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. This applies to all mobile opt-in information regardless of the specific messaging program you sign up for.

Your mobile phone number is used solely to send the text messages you have consented to receive from Your Venue. When you opt in, we may share your number with our messaging service providers (currently Amazon Web Services) and wireless carriers only as necessary to deliver those messages to your phone. These service providers are contractually prohibited from using your information for their own marketing.

Message frequency varies by the events you have purchased tickets for. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile plan.

How to opt out of text messages

You can opt out at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any text message we send you.
  • Contacting us at the email address at the bottom of this page.

After you opt out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further texts from that program. You can resubscribe by replying START to our number, or by completing the opt-in form again on a future ticket purchase.

Reply HELP at any time for support information.

How we share your information

We share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: companies we use to operate our services, including Square (payment processing), Amazon Web Services (hosting and messaging), and email delivery services. These providers may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers: if Your Venue is acquired or transfers assets, your information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information for money or valuable consideration. We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention

We retain your information as long as you have an active relationship with us and as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Consent records for text messaging are retained for the duration required by wireless carrier regulations (currently at least four years after consent revocation).

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you, and the right to know how your information is used. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

You can opt out of text notifications at any time as described above. Opting out of texts does not affect your ability to purchase tickets or receive email confirmations.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
