Weekly Digest

A standing "now playing" roundup, independent of any single title's schedule below.

The digest's image is a background you provide with the day-by-day schedule text drawn on top. Any shape is fine -- it's scaled down to fit within 1024×1024 if larger, without changing its proportions, and the generated digest keeps that same shape. The offsets below say how far to keep the text away from each edge, in pixels, so it doesn't cover a logo or border baked into your background -- re-check them any time you upload a new image, since they're measured against that image's actual size (shown below the preview once uploaded).

No background image set -- a plain white 1080×1080 canvas will be used.

Date, event title, and showtimes each have their own size and weight. Times always render italic to set them apart from the title -- that's not configurable.

Optional static text shown above (header) and below (footer) the auto-generated schedule. "Image" draws it into the generated digest image; "Caption" adds it to the plain-text caption used for every channel (email, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok all share one caption).
