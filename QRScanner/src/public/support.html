Help and support

Contact us

Email us at the address your venue signed up with. Include your Client ID (shown in your welcome email) and what you were trying to do.

Buying a ticket? Contact the venue that sold it; they can resend or refund it.

Common questions
I can't log in

Your Client ID and your main login's username are the same; both are in your welcome email. Staff accounts can use Forgot password on the login page. If you've lost your venue's main login password, email us and we'll reset it.

Square is disconnected, or tickets stopped sending

Open Client Settings and click Reconnect Square, then sign in to Square with your venue's account. This happens if someone removed IndieTix's access in Square. Tickets for orders placed while disconnected aren't sent automatically, so email us and we'll help resend them.

A customer didn't get their ticket

Ask them to check their spam folder for an email from your venue. Find the order on the Orders page to check the email address it was sent to.

Distributor reports

Cinemas' nightly box-office reports go out automatically once the report settings are filled in under Client Settings. If a delivery fails, your venue's contact email gets an alert that says what failed.

Closing your account

Email us from your venue's contact address. We'll confirm, then delete your venue's data. Removing IndieTix in Square stops new orders reaching us right away.

Something not working? Check system status first.
