Privacy Policy

Last updated: [publication date]

Wolffebrothers LLC ("IndieTix," "we," "us," or "our") runs the hosted IndieTix ticketing service for independent cinemas and venues. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices people have.

IndieTix plays two roles:

  • For venues that use IndieTix, we're responsible for the account information they give us, described in "Information about venues" below.
  • For ticket buyers, the venue you bought from is responsible for your information, and its own privacy policy (linked from its ticket pages) applies. We handle that information only on the venue's behalf, as its service provider, and only to run IndieTix for it. If you're a ticket buyer with a question or request about your information, please contact the venue; if you contact us, we'll pass your request to them.

Information about venues

When a venue signs up and uses IndieTix, we collect:

  • Account details: the venue's name, a contact name and email address, and the settings the venue enters (such as its address, sender email and reporting details).
  • Square connection: the venue's Square merchant ID and the access credentials Square issues when the venue connects its account. These are stored encrypted, and used only to work with that venue's own Square account.
  • Staff logins: usernames, email addresses, permissions and passwords for the people the venue gives access. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Technical information: IP addresses, browser details and activity logs from signup, sign-in and use of the service, used for security and troubleshooting.

We use this information to provide the service, keep it secure, send account and service notices, provide support, bill for the service, and meet legal obligations. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with others except the service providers described below or where the law requires.

Email to IndieTix support

When you email IndieTix support (support@indietix.net), we keep your message, your name and email address, any attachments, and our replies, so we can answer you and follow the conversation. Email sent to IndieTix's addresses is also forwarded to our team's mailbox, which Google hosts.

To help us answer faster, the conversation is sent to Anthropic's AI model, Claude, to draft a reply. A person at IndieTix reads, edits and sends every reply; nothing is sent to you automatically. The draft is written from IndieTix's public help information and the emails in your conversation, and not from any venue's or ticket buyer's records.

Information we handle for venues

To run IndieTix for a venue, we process the following on its behalf:

  • Ticket buyer information: names, email addresses and phone numbers from Square orders, what was bought, the tickets issued, and check-in times.
  • Text message consent records: when a buyer opts in to text messages, the phone number, date and time, IP address, browser and the exact consent wording, as carrier rules require, plus any later opt-out.
  • Email list sign-ups: if the venue collects them through IndieTix, the email addresses people submit.

Payment card details are handled by Square and never reach IndieTix.

Each venue's information is stored in its own separate database and storage. We use it only to provide the service to that venue: delivering tickets, sending the messages the venue sets up, checking tickets in, and producing the venue's reports.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging

We do not sell, rent, share or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers, or the fact that someone has opted in to receive text messages, to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. This applies to all mobile opt-in information, for every venue and every messaging program.

Phone numbers are used only to send the text messages people have agreed to receive from the venue, and are shared with our messaging provider (Amazon Web Services) and wireless carriers only as needed to deliver those messages. Anyone can stop texts by replying STOP.

We use these companies to run IndieTix. Each receives only what it needs for the purpose listed:

  • Amazon Web Services (United States): hosting, databases, file storage, email delivery and text message delivery.
  • Square: the venue's own payment, catalog and order system, which IndieTix reads from and writes to on the venue's behalf.
  • Anthropic: if a venue uses IndieTix's AI features. For Insights, the venue's question and the matching records from its database, which can include ticket buyer information, are sent to generate an answer.   • Google: hosts our team's mailbox, which receives copies of email sent to IndieTix's addresses, including support email. If a venue connects a Google Calendar, showtime and event details are also added to that calendar; no ticket buyer information is sent to it.

Cookies and browser storage

IndieTix doesn't use advertising or analytics trackers. Venue staff pages keep a sign-in token and display preferences in the browser's local storage so staff stay signed in and see the columns they chose. Public ticket pages don't use cookies.

Security

We protect information with a separate database for each venue, encrypted connections, encrypted storage of credentials, hashed passwords, role-based staff permissions and a firewall in front of the service. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects a venue's information, we'll notify the venue promptly so it can inform anyone affected, as required by law.

Your choices and rights

Venues can view and update their account information in IndieTix, and can ask us at privacy@indietix.net to export or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to some uses. Ticket buyers should send these requests to the venue; we'll help the venue respond. We won't discriminate against anyone for exercising their rights.

Children

IndieTix is a business service and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we'll update the date above and notify venues by email before the changes take effect.
