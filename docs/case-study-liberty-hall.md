# Liberty Hall: a full box office on Square, with no fees for ticket buyers

**Liberty Hall** is an independent cinema and live music hall in downtown Lawrence, Kansas. It shows first-run and repertory films and hosts concerts and events, all sold through Square.

### The problem

Liberty Hall already ran its concessions and bar on Square, but the ticketing system was another platform with another credit card processor and inventory and an online site.  We wanted to consolidate all these systems if possible and it seemed like square was a good system to build off of, as we also had our online merch site with them.  There were tons of other potential benefits that we came up with if we were able to figure out how to use square to sell our event and cinema tickets.  They were:

Any square terminal can be used to purchase a movie ticket.  Helps distribute and move the customer line.

We can have a more efficient customer experience by allowing the sale of concessions along with a ticket.

By pushing online purchases through square we can add incentives to purchase other items in our store.  We also get a boost simply by having more people on our online site.  For example; buy a ticket to a movie and get 10% off a t-shirt.   

We can build up more “purchasing power” with square and, in the future, lower our credit card fees.

We simplify the number of services we use, and if this goes we will save $5000 a year on our current cinema ticketing subscription.  We may also be able to drop an additional credit processing as well.  

We can use google calendar to post all of our movie information and allow it to be public which would then automatically be searchable by a variety of search engines.  I’ve got a new calendar setup that is intuitive for the end user as well (web browsing person)

We can check sales / update movie information from anywhere as opposed to having to be at liberty to check in / update movie information.

We have full control over this solution.  This is a really big deal in my mind.  If you all have ideas for new functionality etc., I will definitely be open to them, and will most likely be able to make them happen.  This can become a solution that we all build together.  

Zero additional fees beyond the credit card processing fees.  

Once we get over the training hump I also think that we will have a simpler solution for our team.  One interface that they can get used to.  Can make cross training slightly easier as all sales are via square.

If this goes really well then maybe the solution could be used beyond the Hall... a pipe dream  

### What changed

Since April 2025 Liberty Hall has sold every cinema ticket and quite a few smaller event tickets through IndieTix, on the Square catalog, Square Online and Square POS it already used.

- Staff add a film or event once, and IndieTix creates the Square item, ticket types, prices and showtimes, and clears out showtimes that have passed.
- Each purchase becomes a QR-code ticket by email, or by text for buyers who opt in.
- Door staff scan tickets on their phones, and the box office prints ticket labels with one tap.
- Refunds go through Square, and a refunded ticket is refused at the door.
- Box-office reports go to the film-industry reporting services automatically every night.
- Upcoming shows are posted to Facebook and Instagram after staff approve them.

### The results

- **6,700+ tickets** issued
- **$79,000+** in ticket sales, processed on Square
- **575 showtimes and events** across 113 titles
- **$0** in fees charged to ticket buyers
