# Reports and marketing

## Reports
- **Event Report:** tickets sold, revenue and check-ins for one showtime or event, with Export to Excel and CSV.
- **Insights** (admin permission): ask a question in plain English ("how many tickets did we sell last weekend?") and get an answer from the venue's own data. It uses the venue's own AI (Anthropic) API key, entered in Client Settings.
- **Distributor box-office reports** (cinemas, for example comScore/Rentrak): sent automatically every night once the report settings and delivery details are filled in under Client Settings. If a delivery fails, the venue's contact email gets an alert saying what failed.

## Marketing
- **Marketing Schedule:** plan posts for a movie or event (announcement, reminders, opening day) on email, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Presets cover common patterns.
- Drafts are made automatically. **Nothing posts until someone approves it**; approved posts go out at their scheduled time (Kansas/Central time).
- Captions start from the item's Square description, followed by the price and ticket link. They can be edited before approving, or written by AI if turned on for that schedule.
- The picture is the item's image in Square. Instagram pictures are fitted to Instagram's allowed shape automatically.
- A weekly "what's playing" email roundup is also available.
- **Facebook and Instagram** need the venue's Meta access token, Page ID and Instagram account ID in Client Settings; Instagram must be a business or creator account linked to the Facebook Page. **TikTok** needs TikTok's own app approval first.
- **Email List:** manage marketing email subscribers. Ticket emails go to every buyer regardless; marketing emails only go to people on the list, and every one has an unsubscribe link.
