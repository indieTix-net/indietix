# For ticket buyers

Answers for people who bought a ticket from a venue that uses IndieTix.

- **Didn't get your ticket?** Check your spam or junk folder for an email from the venue. Still nothing? Contact the venue that sold the ticket: they can see your order, check the email address on it, and resend it. IndieTix support can't see a venue's orders or resend tickets for a buyer directly.
- **Need a refund or a different showtime?** That's up to the venue that sold the ticket; contact them.
- **Want your ticket by text instead?** If the venue offers texting, the ticket email has a link to switch to text. You can reply STOP to any text to stop them.
- **At the door,** show the QR code on your phone or a printout. Each ticket works once.
- **Your information** belongs to the venue you bought from, and its privacy policy applies. Questions about it go to the venue.
