# Support knowledge pack

What the support inbox's draft replies are allowed to know. Every file here is public (it's in this repository) and is sent, as written, to the model that drafts replies to email sent to support@indietix.net. A person reviews and edits every draft before it's sent; nothing here is sent to anyone automatically.

Keep it to what a venue or ticket buyer could be told. Never add internal notes, account IDs, production details, credentials or anything about a specific venue.

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| `about.md` | What IndieTix is, who it's for, open source and hosted |
| `getting-started.md` | Signing up, the welcome email, logging in, staff accounts |
| `square.md` | Connect Square, selling tickets, adding movies and events, refunds |
| `tickets-and-customers.md` | Ticket emails and texts, resending, transfers, a buyer who didn't get a ticket |
| `at-the-door.md` | The scanner, manual check-in, skeleton keys, printing stubs |
| `reports-and-marketing.md` | Event reports, Insights, distributor reports, marketing posts |
| `texting.md` | Text-message tickets and why they need a 10DLC campaign |
| `account-and-privacy.md` | Settings, web address, privacy requests, closing an account, status page |

Not covered on purpose, so drafts say a person will follow up: pricing and billing, refunds of IndieTix fees, contracts and legal terms, anything about a specific venue's data, and security reports (those go to SECURITY.md's process).
