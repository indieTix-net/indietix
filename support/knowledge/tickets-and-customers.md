# Tickets and ticket buyers

## For ticket buyers
- **Didn't get your ticket?** Check your spam or junk folder for an email from the venue. Still nothing? Contact the venue that sold the ticket: they can see your order, check the email address on it, and resend it. IndieTix support can't see a venue's orders or resend tickets for a buyer directly.
- **Need a refund or a different showtime?** That's up to the venue that sold the ticket; contact them.
- **Want your ticket by text instead?** If the venue offers texting, the ticket email has a link to switch to text. You can reply STOP to any text to stop them.

## For venues
- **A buyer didn't get their ticket:** find the order on the **Orders** page, check the email address on it, fix it if needed (edit the order's contact details), and use **Resend**.
- **Transfer a ticket** to someone else (name, email and optional phone) from the tickets page. The old ticket stops working and a new one is emailed to the new person. Transfers are by email only.
- **Edit an order:** only the buyer's name, email and phone can be changed. Price and showtime changes happen in Square.
- Each ticket is a PDF with a QR code. Ticket PDFs don't include the buyer's personal details.
